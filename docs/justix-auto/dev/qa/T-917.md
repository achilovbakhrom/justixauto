reference string, resolve effective closures/overlaps/hold/resume policy, validate
business data grants or prove bootstrap authority. T-918/T-920 must do that under

and checkpoint transaction or final lease fence. T-922/T-923 remain required.

not authorization. An old consumer skipping INSERT on an already-existing inbox
