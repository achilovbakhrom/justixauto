## Verdict

The exact task commit defines and imports the restricted `/justixauto` vhost
on the approved RabbitMQ 4.3.5 image. The seven service principals are disabled
until credential injection, have no tags or configure permission, can read only
their own inbox/quarantine queues, and can publish only owner-scoped routing
keys to the two approved exchanges.

Live checks confirmed both exchanges and all 14 owner queues are durable, the
queues are quorum queues, and every owner has the expected inbox and quarantine
binding. A mandatory persistent unroutable publication produced
`basic.return 312 NO_ROUTE` and an ACKed publisher confirmation. A persistent
allowed-route probe and all durable topology survived a broker restart.

## Scope and limitations

This task establishes the local topology and least-privilege broker grants. It
does not implement service credential injection, Compose wiring, relay
processes, transactional outbox/inbox storage, duplicate processing, sequence
checkpoints, quarantine-before-ACK, redrive or production deployment. Those
guarantees remain assigned to dependent tasks and are not inferred from
RabbitMQ durability or publisher confirms.

The first sandboxed loopback invocation was blocked by the filesystem/network
sandbox with `connect: operation not permitted`; the authorized loopback rerun
passed. During preparation of the independent temporary probe, its two files
were briefly created in the main checkout by mistake. QA removed only those
files, restored `go.mod` byte-for-byte using `apply_patch`, and verified the
main checkout clean before writing this report.
