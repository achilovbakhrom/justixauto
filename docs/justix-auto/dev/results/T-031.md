## Changed paths and behavior

- `pkg/auth/caller.go`: typed identities for the seven service owners and edge;
  an unforgeable-by-request `Caller`; immutable, concurrent-safe exact
  caller/action/purpose grants; and deny-by-default authorization. Empty,
  whitespace-altered, unknown and wildcard-like requests do not broaden grants.
- `pkg/auth/transport.go`: resolves one enabled service identity from an exact
  configured URI SAN on the verified mTLS peer leaf. Missing/unverified,
  unknown, disabled and ambiguous identities fail closed with typed errors.
  Certificate trust, issuance and rotation remain TLS/deployment concerns; this
  task contains no production certificate or hardcoded production SAN.
- `pkg/auth/transport.go`: detects and rejects spoofed actor, permission and
  internal identity headers on public ingress; can remove them from an isolated
  proxy-header copy without mutating the inbound request. Public
  `X-Context-Revision` and ordinary transport headers are preserved.
- `pkg/auth/caller_test.go`, `pkg/auth/transport_test.go`: exact allow/deny
  matrix, configuration-copy immutability, concurrent readers, missing/unknown/
  disabled/ambiguous peers, leaf-chain binding, actual in-memory mutual TLS
  handshake, and mixed-case/underscore spoof-header cases.
- This result file only; no canonical documents, manifests, service runtime,
  session/JWT logic, production secrets or reference infrastructure changed.

## Limits and blockers

No implementation blocker remains. This package supplies shared admission
mechanics only. Owner adapters must assign their own exact action/purpose grant
configuration and must still validate durable admission references, holder,
company, party, state and revision under their owning tasks. It does not claim
broker delivery guarantees, business authorization, session authentication,
certificate operations or production readiness. Independent QA and coordinator
integration remain pending.
